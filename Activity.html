Introduction to Science

Science is the systematic study of the natural world.It helps us understand how things works and allow us to develop technologies that improve our daily lives.Scientific knowledgeis based on observation,evidence,experimentation,and careful analysis.


The Solar System consists of the Sun and the objects that orbit it.,including planets,moons,asteriods,and comets. Earth is the third planet from the Sun and is the only known planet that supports life.The Earth contains approximately 71% water.Water is represented chemically as H20,while carbon dioxide is represented as CO2.


Environmental Science focuses on the relationship between humans and the natural environment.Climate change, pollution and deforestation are important environmentral concerns.Scientist study these problems and develop solutions to protect the planet,The energy of objects may also be expressed using scientific notation such as 102.
